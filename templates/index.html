Theoretical Proofs & Derivations

Rigorous mathematical foundations underpinning statistical modeling & algorithms.

Given $X_1, X_2, \dots, X_n \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$, the Likelihood Function is:

$$L(\mu, \sigma^2) = \left(2\pi\sigma^2\right)^{-\frac{n}{2}} \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - \mu)^2\right)$$

Taking the natural logarithm (Log-Likelihood $\ell$):

$$\ell(\mu, \sigma^2) = -\frac{n}{2}\ln(2\pi) - \frac{n}{2}\ln(\sigma^2) - \frac{1}{2\sigma^2}\sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - \mu)^2$$

Solving $\frac{\partial \ell}{\partial \mu} = 0$ yields the MLE estimator for mean:

$$\hat{\mu}_{MLE} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n X_i = \bar{X}$$

For the linear model $Y = X\beta + \varepsilon$ with $E[\varepsilon|X] = 0$ and $Var(\varepsilon|X) = \sigma^2 I$, the OLS estimator is:

$$\hat{\beta} = (X^T X)^{-1} X^T Y$$

Proof of Unbiasedness:

$$E[\hat{\beta}] = E[(X^T X)^{-1} X^T (X\beta + \varepsilon)] = \beta + (X^T X)^{-1} X^T E[\varepsilon] = \beta$$

Thus, $\hat{\beta}$ is the Best Linear Unbiased Estimator (BLUE).
